This is where design is meant to go. This is where I strive to be. This is what I want to do in design. Not an agency. Not a random firm that is going to make me design paper products for some unknown company on the West Coast. Not what they teach me in school. I want the teams. The actions. The college sports. The athletes grinning. The fast paced. The new designs. The bold letters. The clean designs. I want all of this. If I was to choose design. I would pick this. And only this. Sports meets design. We should go strive to go away from that but we should strive to go closer. We go to UGA after all. Should we not practice this. Enough with the posters for random companies on the West Coast. I want this. This is what I want.
