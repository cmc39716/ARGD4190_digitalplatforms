Sports

The love for sports is something that has never gone away. From the beginning I knew this is where I was meant to be and I still want to be here so bad. I want to be a full time photographer. This is my passion.The love of the game. It goes deeper than I ever thought that it would. The staged moments don’t need to happen because the action is right in front of you. It is constantly happening. Looking through the camera and I can see everything around me. The desire to never look away. The fact that it is just there. There is not much that needs to be done. Right place. Right time. You learn over time though. Where to sit on the field. Which side of the fence you are allowed to be on. How to protect the one thing that is the most valuable in your life. The thing that makes where you are sitting a reality. It is more than just a game. It is a way of life. The people I have met. The photos I have taken. Love is to shallow to describe this feeling. A greater word is needed. But there is none and will never be one to describe the love. So for now the photos will have to be enough.

Singles

These are just moments that have happened. Small details. Larger photos. Emotions. Raw emotions behind the bigger events. Most of them not staged. A lot of them just in them moment. See the details. The flowers on the boutonniere. The name on the jacket. The texture of the boots. A way to see the details and not think about the bigger issues. Focus. Little things matter.

Portraits

The staged events. The big moments in life. The senior pictures. The forming of a new family unit. The celebration of an engagment. The celebration of an accomplishment that no one else would do. High school dances for the last time. It is not anythingi that you will get back. You ask them to smile. To look our way. To pose so unnaturally that it is awkward for the both of you. Because no one knows how to pose. Not high schoolers. Not boys. Not adults. Not girls. No one does. It is not natural. I do not like posing people just as much as the people do not like posing. To be honest it makes money. It is fun sometimes. If I know the people. And even if it is hard it does make money. Posing is not fun. But it is required. It is needed. There needs to be these moments in life. Where you sit there and fake it. It requires a lot but it needs to be done. It can be done. To remember those celebrations. To remember those moments.
