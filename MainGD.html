Graphic Design

Sports

I never thought I would learn a skill outside of photography. Photography was my life. The only thing that I needed. But that led to more. The love for sports grew into the love for design. Taking the photos that I got to capture and making something with them. I can now do something more with those images I capture on the side of the field. They have a greater purpose. It led me to that internship. It led me to more opportunities. All because of my camera.

Other

Because I decided to learn a new skill there are a lot of random designs that I have created. Ones of food. Logos. My portfolio that I spent so much time on. How was I supposed to know that the first item that I picked would be something that I had to work with for the rest of the semester. A wrench is probably not what I would have picked. I think it did make me stand out though. A lot of people picked the bananas or shoes. Wrench. That one was not easy. But we did it. Spent a lot of time on it. Making things. Remaking those same things. Making sure the lines were perfect so that I could get in. So much time. So much stress. All because at one point I was handed a camera. Now I am an art student. What a life.
