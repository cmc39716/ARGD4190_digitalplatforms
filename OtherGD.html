These were fun projects. For the most part. They got me where I needed to be. I wanted to be in the graphic design world and they got me there. All of the hard work. The late nights. The things hung up on the wall so roomates could judge them. There was so much work that went into these. So many things that I had to work though. So many questions about what color something should be or if the lines matched the other side. To many questions not enough answers. That seems to be how life works though. When do we get answers straight forward. When do things come to life. When we bring them to life. We have to work to do that. So here is my proof. My work. The hard work. The time.
