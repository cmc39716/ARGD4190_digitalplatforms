About

I can't believe I am a senior. I am a student at the University of Georgia, double-majoring in Graphic Design and Religion, with a minor in Sports Management and a certificate in Sports Media from the Grady College of Journalism and Mass Communication. My journey to bridge the worlds of design, photography, and sport with the goal of capturing the emotion of competition and the beauty of storytelling through creative media has brought me so far. I have gained a lot of hands-on experience with The Red & Black, Oconee County High School, and the University of Georgia Athletics, where I have documented the energy and emotion that define athletic moments. I still fully believe in the power of visual storytelling. I have learned even more about how it can be used to elevate the world of sports beyond the field. I hope to continue to build a career in sports photography and graphic design, combining my eye for design with my passion for athletics. I can't believe I have taken all of these photos. I have been to such cool places and worked for such a cool university. I just want to document my progress.
